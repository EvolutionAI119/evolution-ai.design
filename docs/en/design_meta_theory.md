## I. The Concept and Levels of Meta-Theory

### 1.1 Definition of Meta-Theory

**Meta-theory** is "theory about theory": it focuses on how theories are constructed, verified, explained, and applied, exploring the philosophical foundations, cognitive logic, cultural presuppositions, and inherent limitations behind theories. It is prerequisite knowledge for theory construction, containing basic beliefs, ideals of natural order, and the unity of lawfulness and purposefulness.

> Quoted from Zhang Zongdeng, *Design History, Design Criticism, Design Practice: Three Levels of the Meta-Theory of Design Studies*: "Meta-theory is a universally significant knowledge phenomenon and form arising from humanity's innate pursuit of understanding and logical reasoning."

### 1.2 The Four Philosophical Dimensions of Meta-Theory

Based on Pan Lusheng's research on the meta-theory of traditional Chinese artisanal creation and on Love's meta-theoretical hierarchy of design theory, meta-theory contains four philosophical dimensions:

| Dimension | Core question | Western paradigm | Chinese paradigm |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ontology** | What is the essence of the world? | Material reductionism, a mechanical view of the universe | An organic view of ceaselessly generating life; all things are formed by *qi* and resonate with one another |
| **Epistemology** | How is knowledge obtained? | Logical positivism, subject–object dualism | Embodied cognition: taking one's own body as the measure to apprehend nature; mind and matter merging; investigating things to attain knowledge |
| **Methodology** | How is practice conducted? | Transforming nature, instrumental reason, maximizing efficiency | Following the Way of heaven, modeling on heaven and timing, the vessel carrying the Way, skillfully following creation, technique advancing into the Way |
| **Axiology** | What ultimate value is pursued? | Absolute truth, other-worldly salvation | The everyday as the Way; individual life merging into the eternal creative flow of the universe |

### 1.3 The Nine Abstraction Levels of Meta-Theory (Extended Love Framework)

Terence Love's meta-theoretical hierarchy of design theory provides an academic framework for layering the automotive-styling knowledge system:

| Level | Classification | Counterpart in automotive styling design |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | Ontology | Chinese creation philosophy: human–nature co-living, the vessel carrying the Way, spirit resonance |
| **L2** | Epistemology | Styling-cognition theory: the three-layer cognitive structure volume → form/feature → graphic elements |
| **L3** | General design theory | Systems theory of automotive styling: the unity of art and science, uncertainty/complexity |
| **L4** | Theory of the designer's internal process | The designer's "embodied cognition," tacit transmission, cultural intuition |
| **L5** | Design-process structure theory | The styling-development process: front-end research → styling design → engineering development |
| **L6** | Design-method theory | Shape grammars, morphological analysis, typology, R-corner regulation |
| **L7** | Selection-mechanism theory | Design review, customer review, proposal-optimization decision mechanisms |
| **L8** | Element-behavior theory | Styling-feature behavior: feature-line heredity, brand-gene expression |
| **L9** | Initial-concept formation | Styling-imagery naming, color systems ("Geely Cyan"), pattern translation |

### 1.4 The Three Levels of Design-Studies Meta-Theory (Zhang Zongdeng Framework)

Zhang Zongdeng proposes that design-studies meta-theory contains three levels forming a complete disciplinary system:

- **Design history**: plurality, factuality, thoughtfulness — inspiring design practice and furnishing evidence for criticism;
- **Design criticism**: value judgment, aesthetic evaluation, cultural scrutiny — providing value and aesthetic standards;
- **Design practice**: human-centeredness, ecological awareness, creativity — the unity of theory and practice.

> Mapping to automotive styling: the history of Chinese automotive styling (from imitation to autonomy), styling criticism (the aesthetic-segmentation map, the nine principles), and styling practice (development process and technical innovation).

### 2.2 Five New Dimensions (Core of the Autonomous Knowledge System)

#### ⑥ Meta-Theory and Philosophical Roots (New)

**This is the first foundational layer of the entire knowledge system, determining the fundamental logic of "why design this way."**

| Sub-dimension | Content |
|---|---|
| Ontological basis | Human–nature co-living: creation is not conquering nature but participating and belonging; generation through *qi*: all things take form through the gathering and dispersing of *qi*, and styling is its flow and condensation |
| Epistemological basis | Embodied cognition: designers apprehend styling with the body as measure; mind–matter merging: investigating things to attain knowledge rather than purely logical deduction |
| Methodological basis | Skillfully following creation: according with the nature of material, crafting according to material; the vessel carrying the Way: styling bears ethics and value |
| Axiological basis | The everyday is the Way: the elevation of life in daily vehicle use; technique advancing into the Way: technical practice is self-cultivation |

> Core proposition: **Chinese automotive styling design is not an engineering act of "transforming nature" but a creative act of "participating in the cycle of cosmic life."**

#### ⑦ Creation Traditions and Cultural Roots (New)

**Providing "spiritual DNA" for styling design from cultural sources.**

| Sub-dimension | Content |
|---|---|
| **The *Kaogongji* system** | Heaven has its seasons, earth its *qi*, material its beauty, craft its skill — only when all four combine can excellent work be made. Mapped to the car: seasons (the era's context), earth's *qi* (local aesthetics), material beauty (material innovation), craft skill (manufacturing precision) |
| **The *Tiangong Kaiwu* spirit** | Heavenly craft first, human skill second — emphasizing technology following rather than opposing natural law; in the electrification era the "heavenly craft" is the natural character of batteries and electric drive |
| **Translation of Xie He's Six Principles** | Spirit resonance → the vital expression of whole-vehicle volume; bone-method brushwork → the force and structure of feature lines; fidelity to form → bionic form and functional response; coloring according to kind → building a Chinese color system; composition/positioning → the order of proportion and spatial layout; transmission through copying → the generational inheritance of brand styling genes |
| **Chinese-user aesthetic-segmentation map** | North China · bold (Yan-Zhao culture); Sichuan-Chongqing · sharp (Ba-Shu culture); Lingnan · plural (inclusive synthesis); Jiangnan · gentle (scholar tradition); Northwest · vast and plain |
| **The vessel carrying the Way** | Styling is not only aesthetics but a carrier of identity, cultural belonging, and way of life |

#### ⑧ The Styling-Domain Knowledge System (New)

**Based on the academic framework of Zhao Jianghong's *Automotive Styling Design: Theory, Research and Application*, constructing a systematic taxonomy of styling knowledge.**

| Sub-dimension | Content |
|---|---|
| **Contextual knowledge** | Market context, brand context, cultural context — case-based contextual knowledge models |
| **Domain knowledge (object knowledge)** | Acquisition and representation of styling features; conceptual models of the styling domain; case-based qualitative–quantitative transformation |
| **Task knowledge (process knowledge)** | Task decomposition, acquisition, and modeling of the design process; task-based computer-aided conceptual design |
| **Styling-feature system** | Feature lines — the skeleton and information carrier of overall styling; styling genes — generational transmission of core information |
| **Styling Gestalt** | The whole–part cognitive completion relationship; Volume → symbolic/abstract semantics |
| **Styling theme** | Narrative expression of design intent; Treatment → indexical semantics; Graphic → iconic semantics |
| **Three-layer styling cognition** | Volume layer (overall aesthetic cognition) → form layer (styling language and technique) → graphic layer (visual-identification advantage) |

#### ⑨ Design Principles and Value Propositions (New)

**Expanding Geely's nine principles into an industry-level system of Chinese automotive design principles.**

| Principle | Meaning | Theoretical root |
|---|---|---|
| **Rooted in the brand's cultural core** | Design language iterates with the era while its core meaning remains constant | The vessel carrying the Way |
| **Rooted in technological development** | Design must respond to the characteristics of electrification and intelligence | *Tiangong Kaiwu*: heavenly craft first |
| **Rooted in user needs** | Responding to local aesthetics starting from cultural geography | Heaven has seasons, earth has *qi* |
| **Pursuing the aesthetic greatest common divisor** | Finding consensus across plural aesthetics rather than a "unified style" | The everyday is the Way |
| **Craft must carry intent** | Every design arrangement serves both function and intent | Technique advancing into the Way |
| **Countering design entropy** | Keeping judgment consistent in complex environments; constraints are freedom | Creation ethics: the self governs things |
| **Designing the car within the car** | Design is not external "packaging" but participation in product definition | Embodied cognition |
| **Warm order** | An industrial product possesses human warmth; order reaches emotion | Mind ethics: nourishing the heart through technique |
| **Quality determines value** | Refinement and premium quality are the cornerstone of long-termism | Material beauty, craft skill |

> Chen Zheng's three questions for Chinese design: ① Is the cultural core genuinely rooted rather than piled up as elements? ② Can a design paradigm of cultural subjectivity be established? ③ Can manufacturing scale transform into a value paradigm?

#### ⑩ The Chinese Styling Language and Paradigm System (New)

**Turning the subjective cognition of "spirit resonance" into quantifiable, applicable, transmissible styling regulations.**

| Sub-dimension | Content |
|---|---|
| **Chinese R-corner regulation system** | Extracted from curvature characteristics of ancient architecture and traditional objects (porcelain, furniture), turning spirit resonance into transmissible R-corner rules; hundreds of curvature-transition combinations satisfy aerodynamics while retaining the warm texture of Eastern objects |
| **Chinese color system** | Taking water as model, emotion as expression — the "Geely Cyan" system is constructed across four dimensions; breaking beyond Western CMYK/RGB quantification by linking to cultural meaning |
| **Chinese pattern-translation system** | Six cultural themes: landscapes, auspicious symbols, solar terms, calligraphy, objects, folk customs; bringing traditional patterns out of museums into vehicle-use scenarios |
| **Chinese proportion and space paradigm** | "Three-cabin grand space" — boldness, nobility, composure; warm and restrained whole-vehicle proportion |
| **Brand styling-gene map** | The heredity diagram of styling features — from predator appearance to warm gentleman; the generational transmission of brand DNA |
| **Form semantics and light-shadow deconstruction** | Finding form → shaping form → brand-gene symbols; premium and refined form expression |

## VII. Closing: The Vision of China's Autonomous Knowledge System for Automotive Styling Design

China's autonomous knowledge system for automotive styling design is not a "local adaptation" of Western design theory, but a theoretical paradigm with cultural subjectivity growing from the roots of Chinese creation philosophy. Its core logical chain:

> **Meta-theory (ontology/epistemology/methodology/axiology) → creation traditions (*Kaogongji*/*Tiangong Kaiwu*/Xie He's Six Principles) → design principles (nine principles) → styling language (R-corners/colors/patterns/genes) → global aesthetic output**

When more Chinese brands find their own design roots and claims, and when the whole industry jointly builds a complete Chinese design discourse, Chinese design will ultimately transform from a "manufacturing symbol" into a "value paradigm" — this is not one company's solo performance but a collective long march of the entire Chinese brand industry.
